Introduction

Who we are: BROD Traders (Pty) Ltd, trading as LaafStyl, is a South African digital company based in Langebaan, Western Cape (postal code 7357). We operate four products: LaafWeb (web.laafstyl.org), LaafConnect (register.laafstyl.org), LaafShuttle (shuttle.laafstyl.org), and LaafEntertain (entertain.laafstyl.org).

This policy covers how we handle personal information across all these platforms and the main website at laafstyl.org. "Personal information" has the meaning given to it in POPIA — broadly, any information that identifies or could identify a natural person or juristic person.

✅
We are committed to processing your personal information lawfully, fairly, and transparently. We will only collect what we need, keep it only as long as necessary, and protect it with appropriate security measures.

By using any LaafStyl product or service, you consent to the collection and processing of your personal information as described in this policy.

Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on which product you use. Here is what each product may collect:

LaafWeb
Business name, contact details, email address, phone number, physical address, website content and images submitted by the client, and payment information.
LaafConnect
Business WhatsApp number, business details, WhatsApp message content from your customers (for automation purposes), customer names and contact numbers as shared via WhatsApp.
LaafShuttle
Passenger name, contact number, email address, booking details (route, date, time, number of seats), and payment information.
LaafEntertain
Event organiser contact details, business name and address, event information submitted for listing, and enquiry form submissions.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data such as pages visited, time spent on pages, and device/browser type — collected via standard web analytics.
  • IP address and general geographic location (country/city level).
  • Referral source (how you found our website).

What we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect sensitive personal information (such as race, religion, health data, or biometric data) unless you explicitly provide it as part of your business description.
  • We do not collect payment card numbers directly — payments are processed by authorised payment processors.

How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information only for the purposes for which it was collected, or for compatible purposes as permitted by POPIA. Our primary uses include:

  • To provide and deliver our services — setting up websites, activating WhatsApp automation, processing shuttle bookings, and publishing event listings.
  • To communicate with you — responding to enquiries, sending invoices, providing service updates, and offering support.
  • To process payments — issuing invoices and confirming payment for subscriptions and once-off services.
  • To improve our products — analysing usage patterns (in anonymised or aggregated form) to improve our platforms.
  • To comply with legal obligations — maintaining records as required by South African tax and company law.
🚫
We do not use your information for unsolicited marketing. We will only contact you about our services if you have engaged with us or opted in. You may opt out of any communication at any time.

Information Sharing

🔒
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party. Ever.

We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers — trusted third-party providers who assist us in delivering our services, such as web hosting providers, payment processors (e.g. PayFast), and communication platforms (e.g. Meta/WhatsApp Business API). These providers are contractually obligated to protect your information and may only use it to provide services to us.
  • Legal requirements — if we are legally required to disclose information in response to a valid court order, subpoena, or other legal process under South African law.
  • Business transfers — in the unlikely event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of LaafStyl, your information may be transferred to the new entity, subject to the same privacy protections.

We require all third-party service providers to maintain appropriate security measures and to process personal information only on our instructions and in accordance with POPIA.

Cookies

Our websites use cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve your experience and analyse how our platforms are used. Cookies are small text files stored on your device.

Types of cookies we use:

  • Essential cookies — necessary for the basic operation of our websites (e.g. session management for booking forms). These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand how visitors interact with our websites (e.g. pages visited, time on site). We use anonymised data only.
  • Preference cookies — remember your choices and settings for a better experience on return visits.

How to disable cookies:

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies or delete them after each session. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of our websites. For guidance on managing cookies, visit your browser's help section or allaboutcookies.org.

📊
Any analytics data we collect through cookies is anonymised or aggregated. We cannot identify individual users from analytics data.

WhatsApp Communications

This section applies specifically to LaafConnect clients and their customers, as well as to anyone who contacts LaafStyl directly via WhatsApp.

LaafConnect Users (Businesses)

  • By signing up for LaafConnect, you authorise LaafStyl to configure and manage WhatsApp automation on your behalf using the WhatsApp Business API.
  • LaafStyl accesses your WhatsApp Business account solely for the purpose of setting up and maintaining your automation. We do not read, store, or use your customer conversations for any purpose other than delivering the automation service.
  • You are responsible for informing your own customers that they are interacting with an automated system when using your LaafConnect-powered WhatsApp.

Your Customers' Data

  • When your customers interact with your LaafConnect automation, their WhatsApp display names and messages may be processed by our systems to generate automated responses.
  • As a LaafConnect client, you are a responsible party under POPIA for your customers' data. You must ensure your own privacy notice informs customers accordingly.
  • LaafStyl acts as an operator/processor of this data on your behalf.

Direct WhatsApp Contact with LaafStyl

When you contact LaafStyl directly via WhatsApp (063 669 1391), your message and contact number will be saved to allow us to respond and manage our client relationship. This information is treated in accordance with this Privacy Policy.
