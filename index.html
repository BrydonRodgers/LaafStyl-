The four LaafStyl products

Each one stands on its own. Together they cover the basics of running and promoting a local business.

LaafWeb

Websites for small businesses

A clear one-page website for your business, with your services, your contact details and a WhatsApp button. Your site can be in English, Afrikaans, isiXhosa or isiZulu.

LaafConnect

AI assistant for WhatsApp

An assistant that answers the questions customers send to your business WhatsApp, such as prices and hours, and tells you when a customer needs a real person.
